Edenred's customer support team is here to help you with any questions or issues relating to your benefits, cards, wallets, and MyEdenred account. Whether you're experiencing a technical problem with the app, have a question about a transaction, or need help with your card, there are several ways to get in touch. Read on to find the best channel for your situation, and to make sure you have the information ready that the support team will need.

## Accessing the Contact Page in the App

You can reach Edenred's contact details and general FAQs directly within the MyEdenred app, without needing to search online.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Support section">
    In the MyEdenred app, navigate to the **Support** (Apoio) section from the main menu or bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Contacts & General FAQs">
    Select **Contacts & General FAQs** (Página de Contactos e FAQs Gerais). This page displays Edenred's phone number, email address, and website, as well as a set of general FAQs organised by category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to get in touch">
    Select the contact method that suits your situation — phone, email, or the in-app contact form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ways to Contact Support

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="In-App Contact Form" icon="message-lines">
    Submit a support request directly from the **Contacts** page in the app. Describe your issue and Edenred's team will follow up with you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Phone Support" icon="phone">
    Call Edenred Portugal's customer support line. Phone details are displayed on the **Contacts & General FAQs** page within the app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Send an email to Edenred's support address. You'll find the current email address listed on the **Contacts** page in the app.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  The most up-to-date contact details — including phone numbers and email addresses — are always available on the **Contacts & General FAQs** page inside the MyEdenred app or web portal. Contact details may change over time, so it's best to check the app for the current information rather than relying on older sources.
</Info>

## Information to Have Ready

To help the support team resolve your issue as quickly as possible, have the following information ready before you get in touch:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email address" icon="at">
    The email address registered to your MyEdenred account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="NIF (tax number)" icon="id-card">
    Your Portuguese NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal), which is used to link your products to your account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Card or wallet number" icon="credit-card">
    The 16-digit number on your Edenred card, or your Wallet ID — relevant if your question is about a specific product.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Description of the issue" icon="clipboard-list">
    A clear description of what happened, including any error messages you saw, what you were trying to do, and when the problem occurred.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Having these details ready means the team can look up your account immediately and avoid unnecessary back-and-forth.

## Notes on Specific Issue Types

<Note>
  **Card transactions and top-ups**: Edenred card transactions are processed through banking partner infrastructure (Novo Banco for cards; Paytec for wallets). If you're querying a specific transaction, a missing top-up, or a payment that didn't go through as expected, the support team can help investigate — but bear in mind that some queries may require coordination with the relevant processing partner, which can take a little longer to resolve.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Benefit loading queries (e.g. "Why hasn't my balance been updated?")**: The loading of benefits onto your card or wallet is the responsibility of your employer. Edenred processes the load once your employer sends the instruction — MyEdenred cannot initiate or override this process. If you believe a benefit loading is missing or overdue, **contact your employer's HR or payroll team first**. They can confirm whether the loading has been sent to Edenred and on what date to expect it to reflect on your balance.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Do not share your MyEdenred **password** or **PIN** with support agents. Edenred staff will never ask for these details. If you're ever asked to provide them, do not comply and report it immediately.
</Warning>

## Product Conditions and Legal Information

If you're looking for the terms and conditions specific to one of your products, or need to review the general MyEdenred application terms, you can access these directly in the app:

- **Product-specific FAQs and conditions**: Open your product's detail page in the app (tap on the card or wallet from the home screen), then select the **FAQs & Conditions** tab. This tab contains the legal terms and conditions that apply specifically to that product, as well as product-level FAQs.
- **MyEdenred application Terms & Conditions**: Available in the app's **Settings** (Preferências) menu.
- **Privacy Policy**: Also accessible from the **Settings** menu in the app. The Privacy Policy explains how Edenred collects, uses, and protects your personal data in connection with the MyEdenred service.

<Tip>
  You can access the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy at any time, even before contacting support — they're always available in your app's settings without needing to log a request.
</Tip>
