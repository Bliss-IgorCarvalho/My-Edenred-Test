Whether you're just getting started with MyEdenred or running into something unexpected, you'll find answers to the most common questions below. These FAQs cover everything from adding a product and managing your card, to making wallet payments and understanding how your account works. If you can't find what you're looking for here, you can reach Edenred's support team via the [Contact page](/support/contact).

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How do I add a product to my account?">
    Products — Edenred cards and wallets — are linked to your account using your **NIF (tax identification number)**. When you register for MyEdenred, the app checks your NIF and automatically identifies any Edenred products already associated with it.

    You can add a product in two ways:

    - **During registration**: If a product is found matching your NIF, you'll be prompted to add it as part of the onboarding flow.
    - **After registration**: Open the main menu, navigate to **My Benefits** (or **Os Meus Benefícios**), and select the option to add a new product. You'll be guided through a short flow to confirm the association.

    For **cards**, you'll also need your 16-digit card number to complete the linking process. For **wallets**, the Wallet ID is matched automatically via your NIF.

    If no product is found, check that the NIF on your MyEdenred account matches the one your employer has on file — any mismatch will prevent the product from appearing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I find my card in the app?">
    If your Edenred card isn't showing up in the app, the most common reasons are:

    - **NIF mismatch**: Your card is linked to a NIF that differs from the one registered in your MyEdenred account. Contact your employer's HR team to confirm which NIF is on record with Edenred.
    - **Card number not entered**: For Edenred cards, you need to enter the **16-digit card number** found on the front of your physical card to complete the association. Make sure you've completed this step.
    - **Card not yet loaded by your employer**: If your employer has only recently enrolled you in a benefit scheme, it may take a short time before the card appears in the system. Allow one or two business days and try again.

    If the issue persists after checking all of the above, contact Edenred customer support with your NIF and card number ready.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a Card and a Wallet?">
    Edenred offers two types of product, each suited to different benefit types:

    **Card** (Cartão)
    - A physical card (like a payment card) issued and sent to you by Edenred.
    - Identified by a **16-digit card number**.
    - Used at physical points of sale and accepted merchants.
    - Backed by **Novo Banco** for transaction processing.
    - Current card types: **Meal Card** (Cartão Refeição) for meal allowances, and **Flexible Card** (Cartão Flexível) for multi-benefit spending across categories such as health, education, and social support.
    - Supports PIN management directly in the app.
    - Can be shared with an **Associated User** (e.g. a spouse) who gets read-only access to view balance and transactions.

    **Wallet** (Carteira Digital)
    - A purely digital instrument — there is no physical card.
    - Identified by a **Wallet ID**.
    - Payments are made through the app, typically for a specific purpose (e.g. childcare fees or education expenses).
    - Backed by **Paytec** for transaction processing.
    - Current wallet types: **Childcare Wallet** (Wallet Creche) and **Education Wallet** (Wallet Educação).
    - Does **not** support Associated Users — only the account holder can access it.

    Both types of product appear on your MyEdenred home screen and give you full visibility of your balance and transaction history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use my Edenred card with Apple Pay or Google Pay?">
    No. Edenred cards and wallets are **not compatible with Apple Pay or Google Pay**.

    This is a limitation of the Edenred card scheme — unlike Visa or Mastercard, Edenred cards operate on a closed-loop network that is not supported by the Apple Pay or Google Pay platforms. This applies to both physical cards and digital wallets.

    To make payments with your Edenred benefit, you should use your physical card at accepted points of sale (for Cards), or initiate a payment directly through the MyEdenred app (for Wallets).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was I asked for an OTP code when logging in?">
    MyEdenred uses **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** to keep your account secure. As part of this process, you'll occasionally be asked to verify your identity with a **one-time passcode (OTP)** sent to your registered mobile phone number.

    When MFA is triggered depends on how you're accessing the app:

    - **Mobile app**: MFA is required once every **180 days**. If you log in from the same device within this period, you won't be asked again until the period expires.
    - **Web portal**: MFA is required at **every new session**, as the web browser does not retain trusted device status between sessions.

    The OTP is sent via SMS to the phone number you registered when you created your account. If you no longer have access to that number, contact Edenred support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I block my card if it's lost or stolen?">
    If your card is lost or stolen, you can block it immediately from within the MyEdenred app — no need to call support first.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open My Benefits">
        From the home screen, tap on your card to open its detail page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find the block option">
        On the card detail screen, look for the **Block Card** option (sometimes shown as a toggle or a dedicated button).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm the action">
        Confirm that you want to block the card. The card will be suspended immediately, preventing any new transactions.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    You can unblock the card through the same screen if you find it again. If the card has been stolen or you need a replacement, contact Edenred customer support directly.

    <Warning>
      Only the **card holder** (the primary registered user) can block or unblock a card. Associated Users have read-only access and cannot perform this action.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I forgot my password — what should I do?">
    You can reset your password easily from the login screen without needing to contact support.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Go to the login screen">
        Open the MyEdenred app or web portal and navigate to the login page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap 'Recover Password'">
        Below the login form, tap the **Recover Password** (or **Recuperar Password**) link.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your email address">
        Enter the email address associated with your MyEdenred account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your email">
        You'll receive an email with a link to reset your password. Follow the instructions in the email to create a new password.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Log in with your new password">
        Return to the login screen and sign in using your new credentials.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    If you no longer have access to your registered email address, contact Edenred customer support for further assistance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is an Associated User?">
    An **Associated User** (Utilizador Associado) is a secondary person — typically a spouse or partner — who has been granted **read-only access** to view the balance and transaction history of the primary account holder's Edenred card.

    This is useful if, for example, your partner wants to see the remaining balance on your Meal Card before heading out to lunch, without needing to share your login credentials.

    Key points about Associated Users:

    - The feature applies to **Cards only** — Wallets do not support Associated Users.
    - An Associated User can view balance and transactions but **cannot** perform any management actions.
    - The primary account holder (the card owner) must authorise the Associated User from within their own MyEdenred account.
    - The Associated User registers their own MyEdenred account and gains a linked view of the shared card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can an Associated User make payments or manage the card?">
    No. Associated Users have **read-only access** — they can see balance and transaction history, but they cannot take any action on the card.

    Specifically, Associated Users **cannot**:

    - Block or unblock the card
    - Change the card PIN
    - Make payments using the card
    - Remove the card from the account
    - Modify any account or product settings

    All management actions are reserved for the **primary card holder** only. If you need to perform any of these actions, you must log in with the primary account holder's credentials.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I make a payment with my wallet?">
    Wallet payments (for Childcare and Education wallets) are made through the **Pay** tab within the MyEdenred app. Unlike card products, wallets have no physical counterpart — all payments are initiated digitally.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Pay tab">
        From your wallet's detail screen, tap the **Pay** (or **Pagar**) tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the payment details">
        Fill in the required fields:
        - **Student number** — the identifier for the student associated with the benefit.
        - **Establishment** — the school, nursery, or education provider you're paying.
        - **Amount** — the value you wish to pay.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm with an OTP">
        A one-time passcode (OTP) will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter it to authorise the payment.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Payment confirmed">
        Once the OTP is validated, the payment is processed and you'll see a confirmation on screen. The transaction will appear in your wallet's movement history.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Info>
      Only the **account holder** can initiate wallet payments. Associated Users cannot access the Pay function.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Payment Profiles and how do I use them?">
    **Payment Profiles** are saved combinations of an establishment and a student number that you use regularly when making wallet payments. Instead of typing in the same details every time you pay a nursery or school fee, you can save the combination as a profile and select it with a single tap.

    **To create a Payment Profile:**
    1. Go to the **Pay** tab on your wallet.
    2. Enter the establishment and student number as you would for a normal payment.
    3. Choose the option to **Save as Profile** (or similar) before confirming the payment.
    4. Give the profile a name so you can identify it easily later.

    **To use a saved Payment Profile:**
    1. On the Pay tab, select your saved profile from the list.
    2. The establishment and student number fields are pre-filled automatically.
    3. Enter the payment amount and confirm with your OTP as usual.

    Payment Profiles save time if you make recurring payments to the same establishment — for example, monthly childcare fees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does my merchant search show different results from a colleague's?">
    Merchant search results in the **Where to Use** (Onde Utilizar) section are personalised, so it's normal for two users to see different results. The two main factors that affect what you see are:

    - **Your location**: The app uses your device's GPS or a postal code you provide to surface merchants closest to you. If you and a colleague are in different locations, you'll naturally see different nearby results.
    - **Your product type**: The merchant network is filtered by the type of product you hold. A Meal Card holder will see restaurants and food establishments; a Flexible Card holder may see a broader range of categories. Merchants that don't accept your specific product type won't appear in your results.

    If you're looking for a specific merchant that you believe should accept your card, try searching by name rather than browsing by location. You can also suggest a missing merchant through the app's merchant suggestion feature.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my email address?">
    You can update your email address in the **Profile** settings section of the app.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Profile settings">
        Tap your profile icon or navigate to **Profile** (Perfil) in the main menu.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Email">
        Find the email address field and tap it to edit.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your new email address">
        Type in the new email address you'd like to use for your MyEdenred account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Validate your new email">
        A validation message will be sent to your new email address. Open it and follow the link to confirm the change.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Your new email address does **not** take effect until you complete the email validation step. Until then, your old address remains active. Make sure to check your inbox (including spam/junk folders) for the validation message.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my benefits if I delete my account?">
    Deleting your MyEdenred account removes your **app access and account data only** — it does not affect the underlying benefit products themselves.

    Here's what this means in practice:

    - Your Edenred card or wallet remains valid and continues to hold any loaded balance.
    - You can still use your physical card at accepted merchants (for card products) even without an active MyEdenred account.
    - Any future benefit loadings from your employer will still be applied to your card or wallet.
    - If you want to use the app again in the future, you'll need to register a new account and re-add your products.

    If you're concerned about losing access to your balance or transactions before deleting, we recommend exporting or noting your recent transactions beforehand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I registered but can't access the app — why?">
    If you've successfully created a MyEdenred account but are unable to proceed past the initial screens, the most likely cause is that **no product has been linked to your account yet**.

    MyEdenred requires at least one active Edenred product (card or wallet) to be associated with your account before you can access the full app experience. If no product is found during or after registration, the app automatically redirects you to the **Add Product** flow.

    To resolve this:

    1. Follow the prompts to add your product — you'll need your **NIF** and, for cards, your **16-digit card number**.
    2. If no product is found matching your NIF, confirm with your employer's HR team that your benefits have been set up and that the NIF on file is correct.
    3. If your employer has recently enrolled you, allow a short processing period (one to two business days) before trying again.

    <Info>
      If you're certain your product should be linked and the issue persists, contact Edenred customer support. Have your NIF and, if applicable, your card number ready.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>
