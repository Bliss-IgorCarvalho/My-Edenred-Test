You can link your Edenred products to MyEdenred both during the initial registration flow and at any point afterwards from your benefits area. Each product you add gives you visibility into its balance and transaction history directly in the app. You can associate up to **5 products** with a single account.

## Supported product types

MyEdenred supports four types of Edenred products across two categories — physical cards and digital wallets.

| Product | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Cartão Refeição** | Card | A meal card loaded by your employer each period and accepted at restaurants and food retailers |
| **Cartão Flexível** | Card | A flexible benefit card that can be used across a wider range of categories as defined by your employer's benefit policy |
| **Wallet Creche** | Wallet | A digital wallet for childcare expenses, such as nursery or crèche fees |
| **Wallet Educação** | Wallet | A digital wallet for education-related expenses, such as school fees and educational materials |

## How to add a product

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="During Registration">
    Adding your first product is a required step during registration — you cannot access the app without at least one product linked to your account.

    After validating your email and mobile number, you are taken to the **Add Benefits** screen. Follow the steps for your product type below.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select the product type">
        Choose from **Cartão Refeição**, **Cartão Flexível**, **Wallet Creche**, or **Wallet Educação**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the product details">
        - **For a card:** Enter the **16-digit card number** printed on the front of your card.
        - **For a wallet:** Enter your **Wallet ID** and your **NIF** (Tax Identification Number). Both are verified against Edenred's records before the wallet is linked to your account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm and continue">
        Once the product is verified, it is added to your account. You can add up to 3 products during the registration flow itself. If you want to add more, you can do so later from the benefits area.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Products are matched to your account using your **NIF**. Make sure the NIF you entered during registration matches the one on record with your employer, otherwise the product association will fail.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="After Registration">
    Once you're logged in, you can add new products at any time from the **My Benefits** area of the app or web portal.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Go to My Benefits">
        From the home dashboard, navigate to **My Benefits** and tap or click **Add benefit** (or **Add product**).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the product type">
        Choose from **Cartão Refeição**, **Cartão Flexível**, **Wallet Creche**, or **Wallet Educação**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the product details">
        - **For a card:** Enter the **16-digit card number** printed on the front of your card.
        - **For a wallet:** Enter your **Wallet ID** and your **NIF**. Both are verified against Edenred's records before the wallet is linked to your account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="View the product in your carousel">
        After a successful addition, a confirmation screen appears and the new product is immediately available in your product carousel on the dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Products are matched to your account using your **NIF**. If your NIF doesn't match the one associated with the product in Edenred's system, the addition will be rejected.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Cards vs. wallets — key differences

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Physical Cards" icon="credit-card">
    **Cartão Refeição** and **Cartão Flexível** are physical cards issued by your employer. They arrive already activated and work at point-of-sale terminals without requiring the app. Adding them to MyEdenred is optional — but strongly recommended so you can monitor your balance and transaction history in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Digital Wallets" icon="wallet">
    **Wallet Creche** and **Wallet Educação** are purely digital — there is no physical card. Payments are made through the MyEdenred app itself, so you **must** add the wallet to the app to be able to use it. A wallet can only have one account holder and does not support associated users.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## If a card is already registered to another account

If you enter a card number that is already linked to a different MyEdenred account, the app will start the **associated user** flow. This is used when a card is already registered under another account — for example, when a family member wants read-only visibility of a shared card. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the association.

<Tip>
  Keep your physical card handy when adding it — you'll need the full 16-digit number on the front. If your card hasn't arrived yet but your employer has already assigned benefits to your NIF, wait until you have the physical card before completing this step.
</Tip>
