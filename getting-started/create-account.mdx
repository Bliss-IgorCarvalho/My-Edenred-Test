Creating a MyEdenred account is the first step to managing all of your Edenred benefits in one place. Registration takes just a few minutes and walks you through providing your personal details, verifying your email and mobile number, linking your first Edenred product, and setting your communication preferences. Once your account is active, you can check balances, view transaction history, find nearby merchants, and pay with your Edenred wallet — all from the mobile app or the web portal.

<Info>
  MyEdenred does not migrate existing accounts. Even if you had a previous MyEdenred account, you must register fresh after the new version launches. Your Edenred products and balances are unaffected — only the app account needs to be recreated.
</Info>

## Registration steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your personal details">
    On the registration screen, fill in the following fields:

    | Field | Required | Notes |
    |---|---|---|
    | Email address | Yes | Used to log in and receive account notifications |
    | Password | Yes | Must meet the platform's complexity requirements |
    | Full name | Yes | |
    | NIF (Tax Identification Number) | Yes | Used to match Edenred products to your account |
    | Postal code | Yes (at least one) | Enter your work or home postal code — or both. Used as a location fallback in the merchant finder when GPS permission is not granted. |
    | Date of birth | No | Optional; used for marketing segmentation |

    <Note>
      Your **NIF** is the key that links your Edenred products to your account. Make sure you enter it exactly as it appears on your tax documents — any mismatch will prevent products from being associated correctly.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate your email address">
    After you submit your personal details, MyEdenred sends a verification message to the email address you provided. Open your inbox, find the email from Edenred, and click the confirmation link.

    - The link is valid for a limited time. If it expires before you click it, return to the app and request a new one.
    - Check your spam or junk folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
    - You cannot proceed to the next registration step until your email address is confirmed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate your mobile number via SMS">
    Once your email is verified, MyEdenred sends a 5-digit OTP (one-time passcode) by SMS to the mobile number you entered.

    - Enter the code on the verification screen before it expires (codes are valid for **10 minutes**).
    - If you don't receive the SMS, tap **Resend code** — you can request a new code every 60 seconds.
    - After 3 incorrect attempts the verification is locked for **15 minutes**, then unlocks automatically.
    - Requesting a new code instantly invalidates any previously sent code — only the most recent code is valid.

    <Note>
      Providing a mobile number is **mandatory** for all new registrations. Your number is used for SMS-based multi-factor authentication each time you log in.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first Edenred product">
    Before you can access the app, you must link at least one Edenred product — a physical card or a digital wallet. You can add up to 5 products in total.

    **To add a card (Cartão Refeição or Cartão Flexível):**
    Enter the 16-digit number printed on the front of your card.

    **To add a wallet (Wallet Creche or Wallet Educação):**
    Enter your Wallet ID and your NIF. Both are validated against Edenred's records before the wallet is linked.

    See [Add an Edenred Card or Wallet](/getting-started/add-product) for full details on each product type.

    <Note>
      If you remove all products from your account after registration, the account becomes inaccessible. The next time you log in you will be taken directly to the add-product flow before you can use any other features.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your marketing preferences">
    At the end of the registration flow, you are asked whether you want to receive marketing communications from Edenred — for example, promotional offers and partner updates.

    - Accepting is **entirely optional**. Refusing does not affect your ability to use the app.
    - Service notifications (such as scheduled maintenance alerts) are sent regardless of this preference.
    - You can change this setting at any time from your profile after registration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up PIN and biometric unlock">
    As the final step of onboarding, you are prompted to set up a **PIN** and, if your device supports it, **biometric authentication** (fingerprint or Face ID). These give you a fast, secure way to unlock the app on future visits without entering your full password.

    - Your PIN must be set during onboarding — you can update it later from your profile settings.
    - Biometric unlock is optional but strongly recommended for day-to-day convenience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After registration

Once all steps are complete, your account is active and you are taken to the app dashboard. From there you can view your product balances, browse your transaction history, and find merchants that accept your Edenred card or wallet.

<Tip>
  If you added a postal code during registration, the **Where to use** feature will automatically use it as your location when you haven't granted GPS permission to the app — so you'll still see relevant nearby merchants right away.
</Tip>
