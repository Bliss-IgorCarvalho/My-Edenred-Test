MyEdenred is your personal hub for all Edenred benefits. Whether your employer provides a Cartão Refeição for daily meals, a Cartão Flexível for healthcare and education, a Wallet Creche for childcare payments, or a Wallet Educação for school expenses — MyEdenred brings everything together in one place, available on iOS, Android, and the web.

## Available products

| Product | Type | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Cartão Refeição** | Card | Daily meal subsidies at restaurants and supermarkets |
| **Cartão Flexível** | Card | Multi-benefit: health, education, and social support |
| **Wallet Creche** | Digital wallet | Childcare payments for pre-school children |
| **Wallet Educação** | Digital wallet | Educational expenses for school and university students |

## Platforms

MyEdenred is available on:

- 📱 **iOS** — download from the App Store
- 📱 **Android** — download from Google Play
- 🌐 **Web portal** — access from any browser

<Note>
  The web portal offers the same features as the mobile app. Your login credentials work on all platforms simultaneously.
</Note>

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Register with your email, personal details, and NIF (tax number) at [getting-started/create-account](/getting-started/create-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your Edenred product">
    Add at least one card or wallet using your card number or NIF. See [Add a Product](/getting-started/add-product).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and explore">
    Sign in with email and password. MFA via OTP keeps your account secure. See [Logging In](/getting-started/login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start managing your benefits">
    Check balances, view transactions, pay with wallets, and explore the merchant network.
  </Step>
</Steps>
