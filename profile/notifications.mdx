MyEdenred keeps you informed about everything that matters to your benefits — from new benefit loads to important account updates — through two complementary channels: **push notifications** delivered directly to your device, and an **in-app notification inbox** where every message is permanently stored for you to read at your own pace.

## Your Notification Inbox

Your in-app inbox is always accessible from the **bell icon** in the top header of the app. It lists every notification you've received, with the most recent at the top.

Each entry in the inbox shows:
- **Title** — a short summary of the notification
- **Date** — when the notification was received
- **Read/unread status** — unread notifications are visually highlighted so you can spot new messages at a glance
- **Message preview** — an excerpt of the notification content

<Note>
  Every push notification you receive on your device always has a **corresponding in-app message** in your inbox. This means you'll never miss a notification even if you dismissed the push alert — you can always find the full message in the inbox. The reverse is not always true: some in-app messages exist without an associated push notification.
</Note>

## Reading a Notification

Tap any notification in the inbox to open its full detail view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the notification inbox">
    Tap the **bell icon** in the app header to open your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a notification">
    Tap the notification you want to read. This opens the full message and marks it as read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow any links">
    Some notifications include an actionable link. This may be a **deep link** that takes you directly to a specific feature or screen within the app, or an **external URL** that opens in your device's browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Push Notifications

Push notifications are delivered to your device by the operating system, appearing as standard system alerts even when the app is closed or running in the background.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    MyEdenred uses **Apple Push Notification Service (APNs)** to deliver alerts on iPhone and iPad. You must grant notification permissions to the MyEdenred app in **iOS Settings → Notifications → MyEdenred** for push notifications to appear.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    MyEdenred uses **Google Mobile Services** to deliver push notifications on Android devices. Make sure notifications are enabled for MyEdenred in **Android Settings → Apps → MyEdenred → Notifications**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Keep push notifications enabled so you're instantly alerted when your benefits are loaded, when your account requires attention, or when Edenred has important news for you. If you miss a push alert, the message is always waiting for you in your in-app inbox.
</Tip>

## Pop-Up Messages

In addition to the notification inbox and push alerts, MyEdenred displays **pop-up messages** for important announcements. These appear automatically when you navigate to certain screens — such as the home screen, login screen, or benefits overview — without requiring you to open the inbox.

Pop-ups can include:
- A **title** and **description**
- An **image** or banner
- A **button** linking to a feature in the app or an external page

Some pop-ups appear only **once** and do not return after you view them. Others include a **"Don't show again"** option so you can dismiss them permanently after the first view.

<Info>
  Pop-up messages are configured and sent by Edenred — you cannot disable them entirely, but dismissing a pop-up with the "Don't show again" option prevents that specific message from appearing again.
</Info>

## Deleting Notifications

You can remove individual notifications from your inbox when you no longer need them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the notification inbox">
    Tap the **bell icon** in the app header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete a notification">
    Find the notification you want to remove and tap the **delete** action for that item. The notification is removed from your inbox immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Deleting a notification from your inbox removes it permanently. You won't be able to retrieve it afterwards, so make sure you've noted any important information — such as a link or reference number — before deleting.
</Note>
