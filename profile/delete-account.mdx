If you no longer want to use MyEdenred, you can permanently delete your account at any time directly from the app's profile settings — no need to contact support by email. Deleting your account removes your login credentials and all associated personal data from the MyEdenred platform, while leaving your underlying Edenred cards and benefits entirely intact.

## What Happens When You Delete Your Account

Before you proceed, make sure you understand exactly what account deletion does and does not affect:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="What IS deleted" icon="trash">
    - Your MyEdenred login credentials (email and password)
    - Your profile data (name, date of birth, postal code, phone number)
    - Your notification history and in-app messages
    - Your app preferences and settings
    - Associated user links tied to your account
  </Card>
  <Card title="What is NOT affected" icon="shield-check">
    - Your Edenred cards — they continue to work at the point of sale
    - Your Edenred wallets — balances remain available
    - Your employer-issued benefits — they remain active
    - Your ability to re-register with the same NIF in the future
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Account deletion is **permanent and cannot be undone**. Once confirmed, all your MyEdenred profile data, notification history, and app preferences are immediately and irreversibly erased.
</Warning>

## Steps to Delete Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Tap the profile icon in the app or navigate to **Profile** from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Account settings">
    Scroll down and tap **Account** or **Settings** within your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Delete Account'">
    Select **Delete Account** from the list of options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation screen">
    The app displays a summary of what will be deleted. Review it carefully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deletion">
    Tap **Confirm** (or the equivalent confirmation button) to proceed. Your account is permanently deleted immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Re-Registering After Deletion

Deleting your account does not impose any restrictions on creating a new one. You can register again at any time using the same NIF — there are no legal or technical barriers to re-registration.

<Info>
  When you re-register, you start with a fresh account. You'll need to add your Edenred cards and wallets again, as your previous product associations are not retained after account deletion.
</Info>

## Account Deletion Is Not the Same as Cancelling Your Benefits

Deleting your MyEdenred account removes your access to the app and portal, but it does **not** cancel your Edenred products or benefits. Your cards and wallets remain active and usable at point-of-sale terminals.

<Note>
  If you want to cancel your Edenred benefits entirely — for example, because you've left your employer or no longer want the product — contact your **employer's HR department** or reach out to **Edenred customer support** directly. Benefit cancellation is a separate process managed outside the MyEdenred app.
</Note>

## Account Deletion vs. Right to Be Forgotten (GDPR)

Deleting your account through the app is a self-service action that removes your MyEdenred profile data. This is **not** the same as a formal GDPR right-to-erasure (right to be forgotten) request, which may require additional processing and case-by-case review by Edenred's Data Protection Officer.

<Info>
  If you need to submit a formal GDPR erasure request, contact Edenred's DPO through the official data protection contact channel. A formal erasure request may involve steps beyond simple account deletion, such as the removal of data held in backend systems and audit logs.
</Info>
