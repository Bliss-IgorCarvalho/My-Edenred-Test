Your profile is the central place where MyEdenred stores the personal details used across all features — from benefit management to merchant discovery. Keeping this information accurate ensures you get the best experience from the app and that all communications reach you reliably.

## What's in Your Profile

When you open your profile, you'll see the following details:

| Field | Editable? |
|---|---|
| Full name | ✅ Yes |
| Date of birth | ✅ Yes |
| Postal code | ✅ Yes |
| NIF (Tax Identification Number) | ❌ Read-only |

## Editing Your Basic Details

You can update your **name**, **date of birth**, and **postal code** directly — no extra verification is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Tap the profile icon in the app or navigate to **Profile** in the top menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the field you want to change">
    Select the name, date of birth, or postal code field to enter edit mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new value and save">
    Type your updated information and confirm. The change takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your **postal code** up to date. MyEdenred uses it to discover nearby merchants and benefits when your device's GPS is unavailable or turned off.
</Tip>

## Changing Your Email Address

Your email address is used for login and all account communications, so updating it requires an extra validation step to confirm you own the new address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Profile → Email">
    Tap **Email** in your profile settings to open the email change screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new email address">
    Type the new address you'd like to use and submit the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your new inbox for the validation link">
    MyEdenred sends a confirmation link to the **new** email address you entered. Open that email and click the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validation complete">
    Once you click the link, your new email address becomes active for login and communications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Until you click the validation link in your new inbox, your **old email address remains fully active** for login and all communications. The change only takes effect after you confirm the new address. There is currently no time limit on the validation link, but be sure to complete the step promptly to avoid confusion.
</Warning>

## Changing Your Phone Number

Your phone number is used for SMS-based authentication. Updating it requires you to verify the new number with a one-time code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Profile → Phone Number">
    Tap **Phone Number** in your profile settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new number">
    Type the new mobile number you want to associate with your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the OTP sent by SMS">
    MyEdenred sends a one-time password (OTP) to the **new number** via SMS. Enter this code in the app to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Number updated">
    Once the OTP is validated, your new number is saved immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you don't enter the OTP, your phone number is **not changed** — the previous number stays in place. There is no "pending" state for phone number changes: the update either completes immediately upon OTP validation or not at all.
</Note>

## Why Your NIF Is Read-Only

Your NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal) is displayed in your profile for reference but cannot be edited directly. MyEdenred uses your NIF to match you to your Edenred products and benefits — changing it incorrectly could break that link. This field is only shown when you have a Wallet associated with your account.

<Info>
  If your NIF was entered incorrectly during registration, contact Edenred support to have it corrected. Do not attempt to work around this by deleting and re-creating your account without speaking to support first.
</Info>

## Managing Your Marketing Preferences

You can control whether you receive marketing communications from Edenred directly from your profile. A toggle in the profile area lets you opt in or opt out at any time. Changes take effect immediately.

<Note>
  Marketing preferences are separate from transactional notifications (such as benefit loading alerts), which you will always receive regardless of your marketing opt-in setting.
</Note>
