The **Where to Use** section of MyEdenred shows you every partner merchant that accepts your Edenred products — restaurants, grocery stores, childcare centres, schools, and more. Results are automatically filtered to match the products linked to your account, so every merchant you see is one you can actually pay at. You can browse by scrolling a list, searching by name, or exploring an interactive map.

## How Your Location Is Determined

MyEdenred uses the best available location signal to sort merchants by distance, following this priority order:

1. **GPS position** — if you have granted location permission to the app, MyEdenred uses your real-time coordinates for the most accurate results.
2. **Work postal code** — if GPS is unavailable or not permitted, MyEdenred falls back to the work postal code saved in your profile.
3. **Home postal code** — if no work postal code is set, your home postal code is used as the reference point.

<Tip>
  Grant location access to MyEdenred for the most accurate nearby results. You can update the app's location permission at any time in your device settings.
</Tip>

## Browsing Merchants

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="List View">
    The list view gives you a paginated, distance-sorted directory of partner merchants.

    - Results are ordered from nearest to furthest based on your location (see above).
    - Each entry shows the merchant's **name**, **address**, **distance from you**, **category**, and a **promotion badge** if an active offer is available.
    - The list loads approximately **20–30 merchants per page**. Scroll to the bottom to load the next page.
    - Only merchants that accept your specific product type are shown — you will never see a merchant you cannot use.

    Tap any merchant in the list to open its [detail page](#merchant-detail).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Map View (Cards only)">
    Card holders can switch to an interactive **Google Maps** view that plots nearby merchants as pins on a map.

    - Pins are clustered when many merchants are close together — tap a cluster to zoom in and separate them.
    - On the **web portal**, the list and map are displayed side by side. On **mobile**, you toggle between the two views using the view-switcher button.
    - If you pan or zoom the map to a new area, tap **Search this area** to reload results for the region currently visible on screen.

    <Note>
      Map view is available for **card products only** (e.g. Cartão Refeição, Cartão Flexível). Wallet products (Wallet Creche, Wallet Educação) do not have a map view — use the list view to find accepted establishments.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Searching and Filtering

Use the search bar and filter options at the top of the merchant list to narrow down results.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cards">
    - **Search** by merchant name or area to jump directly to a specific location.
    - **Filter by category** to show only a particular type of merchant, such as restaurants, grocery stores, or bakeries. The available categories are determined by the partner network data and may vary.
    - **Filter by promotion** to see only merchants with an active Edenred offer.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Wallets">
    - **Search by name** to find a specific school or childcare centre.
    - **Search by NIF** (tax identification number) if you know the establishment's fiscal number.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Merchant Detail

Tap any merchant in the list to see its full detail page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cards">
    The card merchant detail page includes:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Characteristics" icon="tag">
        Merchant category, accepted products, and any active promotions or special offers.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Opening Hours" icon="clock">
        The merchant's regular trading hours, organised by day of the week.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Address & Map" icon="map-pin">
        A static map image of the merchant's location. Tap **Get Directions** to open Google Maps with a pre-set route from your current position to the merchant.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Photos" icon="image">
        Photos of the merchant provided through the partner network.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Wallets">
    The wallet establishment detail page shows a simplified view with the establishment's name, address, and contact information. A map view is not available for wallet establishments.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Cards vs. Wallets — Key Differences

| Feature | Cards | Wallets |
|---|---|---|
| Data source | MyGon partner network | Edenred systems (real-time) |
| Map view | ✓ Available | ✗ Not available |
| Search by NIF | ✗ | ✓ |
| Category filters | ✓ | ✗ |
| Promotion indicators | ✓ | ✗ |
| Merchant detail richness | Full (hours, photos, map) | Basic (name, address, contact) |

<Note>
  Merchant data for card products comes from the **MyGon** partner network and is refreshed periodically — it is not updated in real time. If a merchant has recently joined or left the network, it may take some time for the change to appear in the app.
</Note>
