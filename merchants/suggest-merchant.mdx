If a shop, restaurant, school, or childcare centre you visit regularly isn't yet part of the Edenred partner network, you can suggest them directly from the MyEdenred app or web portal. Edenred's merchant acquisition team reviews every submission and contacts the merchant on your behalf — growing the network for you and every other Edenred benefit holder.

## How to Submit a Suggestion

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Where to Use">
    Open the **Where to Use** section from the main menu. This is the same section you use to browse the merchant network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Suggest a Merchant">
    - If you are on a **card product** (e.g. Cartão Refeição or Cartão Flexível), the **Suggest a Merchant** option appears at the **top** of the merchant list.
    - If you are on a **wallet product** (e.g. Wallet Educação), the option appears at the **bottom** of the establishment list.

    Tap it to open the suggestion form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the merchant details">
    Complete the form with as much information as you have:

    | Field | Details |
    |---|---|
    | **Merchant NIF** | The merchant's Portuguese tax identification number (NIF). This helps Edenred identify and contact the correct business. |
    | **Merchant name** | The trading name of the shop, restaurant, school, or other establishment. |
    | **Contact** | A phone number or email address for the merchant, if you have it. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Review your entries and tap **Submit**. You will see a confirmation message in the app letting you know whether your suggestion was received successfully.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens After You Submit

Once you submit the form, your suggestion is sent directly to Edenred's commercial team via an integrated Salesforce workflow. From there:

1. The team reviews the submission to confirm the business details.
2. Edenred reaches out to the merchant to discuss joining the partner network.
3. If the merchant agrees to join, they go through the standard onboarding process.

<Note>
  Submitting a suggestion does not guarantee that the merchant will join the network. The final decision rests with the merchant and Edenred's commercial team. You will not receive individual follow-up notifications on the outcome of your specific suggestion, but approved merchants will appear in the network as soon as they are onboarded.
</Note>

## Availability

The **Suggest a Merchant** feature is available for both card and wallet products. It may be enabled or disabled periodically by Edenred — if you cannot see the option in the app, it may be temporarily unavailable in your region or product type.

<Tip>
  Every suggestion you submit helps Edenred identify demand in your area. The more users suggest a particular merchant, the stronger the signal for the commercial team to prioritise that outreach. It is one of the most direct ways you can help grow the network for everyone.
</Tip>
