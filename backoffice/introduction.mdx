The **MyEdenred Backoffice** is the internal management portal that sits behind the MyEdenred mobile app and web portal. It gives Edenred Portugal's operational teams a single, secure interface to manage end-user accounts, push communications, curated content, and Backoffice user administration — all from a desktop browser during normal working hours.

## Who uses the Backoffice

Two internal teams work in the Backoffice, each with their own focus area and role-based access:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Marketing & Media" icon="megaphone">
    Creates and sends push and in-app notification campaigns, defines user segments for targeted communications, manages banners, highlights, perks, FAQs, and all legal and editorial content surfaced in the app and web portal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customer Support (Call Center)" icon="headset">
    Manages end-user MyEdenred accounts, assists users with product and card issues, resets access credentials, updates personal details, and reviews timestamped activity logs to investigate support cases. This team also includes Edenred's external Contact Center partners.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Key areas of the Backoffice

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **User Management** | Search, view, edit, and support MyEdenred end-user accounts |
| **Notifications** | Create and send push and in-app notification campaigns |
| **Segments** | Group users by product, region, or behaviour for targeted communications |
| **Content (CMS)** | Manage banners, perks, FAQs, and legal documents shown in the app |
| **Collaborators** | Manage Backoffice operator accounts, roles, and access permissions |

## Logging in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Backoffice URL">
    Navigate to the Backoffice in your desktop browser. The Backoffice is designed for desktop use and is accessed during office hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type your **username** and **password** on the login screen, then click **Sign In**.

    <Note>
      The Backoffice login uses a **username** (not an email address). If you forget your password, you cannot reset it yourself — contact your Administrator, who will set a new password for you manually.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your workspace">
    After successful authentication, you land on the Backoffice home screen. The sections visible in the navigation menu reflect your assigned role and permissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  There is currently no multi-factor authentication (MFA) step on the Backoffice login. If your organisation's security policy changes, your Administrator will communicate any new requirements.
</Info>

## Logging out

To end your session, click your profile or account menu in the top navigation bar and select **Logout**. You are redirected to the login screen and your session is closed.

<Warning>
  Always log out when you leave your workstation, especially if you share a computer or work in a shared office environment. There is no confirmed automatic session timeout, so leaving a session open unattended poses a security risk.
</Warning>
