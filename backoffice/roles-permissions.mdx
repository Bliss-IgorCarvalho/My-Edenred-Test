The MyEdenred Backoffice uses role-based access control to ensure that every operator can only reach the areas relevant to their day-to-day work. Roles are assigned when a Backoffice account is created and can only be changed by an Administrator. Permissions are fixed by the system — they are not individually configurable.

## Role overview

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Full access to all Backoffice areas |
| **Administrator Call Center** | User Management and Collaborator Management |
| **Call Center** | User Management only |
| **Marketing** | Notifications, Segments, and Content (CMS) |

<Info>
  Both Edenred's internal Customer Support team and external Contact Center partners use the Backoffice. External agents are assigned the **Call Center** role, giving them access to User Management only.
</Info>

## What each role can do

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Administrator">
    As an Administrator, you have unrestricted access to every section of the Backoffice. Your responsibilities typically include:

    - **User Management** — search, view, edit, suspend, and reactivate MyEdenred end-user accounts
    - **Notifications** — create, schedule, and send push and in-app notification campaigns
    - **Segments** — build and manage user segments for targeted communications
    - **Content (CMS)** — manage banners, highlights, perks, FAQs, and legal documents
    - **Collaborator Management** — create, edit, suspend, and delete Backoffice operator accounts
    - **Role assignment** — set and change roles for all Backoffice collaborators
    - **Password management** — reset passwords for any Backoffice collaborator who cannot log in

    <Tip>
      As an Administrator, you are responsible for the health and security of all Backoffice operator accounts. Deactivate accounts promptly when a collaborator leaves or changes role.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Administrator Call Center">
    As an Administrator Call Center, you have access to operational support areas and team management:

    - **User Management** — full access to search, view, edit, suspend, and reactivate MyEdenred end-user accounts
    - **Collaborator Management** — create and manage Backoffice operator accounts within your team's scope
    - All the same user-facing support actions as the Call Center role (see below)

    <Note>
      The Administrator Call Center role is designed for team leads or supervisors who need to manage their agents' accounts in addition to handling user support cases.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Call Center">
    As a Call Center operator, you work exclusively in the **User Management** section. Your access includes:

    - Search for MyEdenred end-user accounts by name, email, NIF, or card/wallet number
    - View complete user profiles including personal details, account status, and consent settings
    - Edit personal data (name, email, date of birth, postcode)
    - Send a password recovery email or re-send the email validation message
    - Suspend or reactivate a user account
    - Delete a user's phone number (the user will be prompted to re-verify a new number at next login)
    - View all cards and wallets linked to a user's account
    - Block or unblock a user's card or wallet
    - Remove a product from a user's account
    - View the user's associated users and remove them if needed
    - Review the timestamped activity log for any user

    <Warning>
      You cannot initiate payments, change PINs, or perform any financial operation on behalf of a user. If a card or wallet requires financial intervention, this must be escalated to Edenred's backend teams.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Marketing">
    As a Marketing operator, you work in the communications and content areas of the Backoffice. Your access includes:

    - **Notifications** — create, configure, and send push and in-app notification campaigns to selected user segments
    - **Segments** — create and manage user segments by applying filters (product type, region, account status, opt-in) or by uploading a file with product IDs or email addresses
    - **Content (CMS)** — manage banners, carousel highlights, perks and advantages, FAQs, and legal documents displayed in the MyEdenred app and web portal

    <Note>
      The Marketing role does not give you access to individual user accounts or personal data. Segment creation works on aggregated groups — you define the audience without viewing individual user records.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Managing collaborator accounts

Only **Administrators** (and Administrator Call Center, within their scope) can create and manage Backoffice operator accounts.

### Creating a new collaborator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Collaborators">
    In the main navigation, open the **Collaborators** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New">
    Click the **New** button at the top of the collaborator list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the account details">
    Complete the creation form:

    - **Name** — the collaborator's full display name
    - **Username** — the login identifier (not an email address)
    - **Password** — set a temporary password manually or use the auto-generate option
    - **Role** — select one of: Administrator, Administrator Call Center, Call Center, Marketing

    <Note>
      There is no email activation flow. The account is created in an **active** state immediately. Share the credentials with the collaborator securely and instruct them to change their password from their profile on first login.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The new collaborator appears in the list and can log in right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Searching and filtering collaborators

The collaborator list gives you a table with these columns: **Name**, **Role**, **Registration date**, **Last login**, and **Status**. Use the available filters to narrow the list:

- **Role** — filter by Administrator, Administrator Call Center, Call Center, or Marketing
- **Status** — Active or Suspended
- **Registration date** — date range picker
- **Last login** — date range picker

### Editing a collaborator

Click any collaborator's name to open their detail page. From here you can:

- **Edit** their name, username, password, or role
- **Suspend** their account to immediately block access without deleting the record
- **Delete** their account to permanently remove it from the system

<Warning>
  Deleting a collaborator account is irreversible. If the person may return or the account may be needed for audit purposes, prefer **Suspend** over **Delete**.
</Warning>

### Resetting a password

If a collaborator is locked out, open their detail page and use the **Edit** action to set a new password manually. There is no automated password recovery flow for Backoffice accounts — the Administrator is the only recovery path.

## Managing your own profile

Any Backoffice collaborator — regardless of role — can view and update their own profile from the account menu in the top navigation bar.

From your profile screen you can:

- View your **name**, **username**, and assigned **role** (role is read-only — only an Administrator can change it)
- Edit your **name** and **username**
- Change your **password**

<Tip>
  Update your password regularly and never share it with colleagues. If you believe your credentials have been compromised, contact your Administrator immediately to have your password reset.
</Tip>
