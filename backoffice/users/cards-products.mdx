From any user's profile in the Backoffice, you can inspect every Edenred product linked to their account — including cards, wallets, balances, recent transactions, and associated users. This gives you the full picture needed to diagnose product-related support cases quickly, without asking the user to send screenshots or contact other teams.

<Info>
  The Cards & Products view is available within the user profile to **Administrator**, **Administrator Call Center**, and **Call Center** roles. You access it directly from the user's detail page — there is no standalone products section in the main navigation.
</Info>

---

## Products tab

When you open a user's profile, navigate to the **Cards** (Cartões) tab. The tab presents all products linked to the account in a card carousel — the same layout the user sees in the MyEdenred app.

### What is shown per product

Each product card displays:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Product name** | E.g. Cartão Refeição, Cartão Flexível, Wallet Creche, Wallet Educação |
| **Product type** | Card (physical) or Wallet (digital) |
| **Card number / Wallet ID** | 16-digit number for cards; alphanumeric ID for wallets |
| **Status** | Active or Blocked |
| **Current balance** | Live balance pulled from the respective backend system |

<Note>
  Balance and status data is retrieved in real time from the financial systems that manage each product type. If a balance appears stale or inconsistent, the issue lies with the upstream financial platform — the Backoffice displays exactly what it receives.
</Note>

## Actions you can take on a product

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="View details and balance">
    Click a product card to open its full detail view. You can see:

    - Product name, type, and identifier
    - Current balance
    - Account status (active or blocked)
    - Whether the product is a Card or Wallet

    This information is read-only — you cannot change product attributes such as the card number or wallet ID.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="View recent transactions">
    Within the product detail, open the **Movements** tab to see the user's recent transaction history for that product. Each entry shows:

    - **Date and time** of the transaction
    - **Merchant name** or description
    - **Amount** (debit or credit)
    - **Transaction status**

    Use this view to verify whether a specific payment went through, identify unexpected charges, or confirm a balance top-up was received.

    <Note>
      You are viewing the transaction history in a read-only, support context. You cannot reverse, void, or dispute transactions from the Backoffice. Financial operations must be escalated to Edenred's operations team.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Block or unblock a card">
    If a user reports a lost card or a suspicious transaction, you can block the product directly from the Backoffice:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the product">
        From the user's **Cards** tab, click the product you want to act on.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Block or Unblock">
        Use the **Block** or **Unblock** action button on the product detail screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm the action">
        Confirm your intent in the confirmation dialog. The status change takes effect in real time — the product cannot be used for transactions while blocked.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Blocking a card is a real financial action. It immediately prevents the user from making any purchases with that card or wallet until you or the user unblocks it. Only block a product when there is a clear support reason to do so.
    </Warning>

    <Info>
      Whether a block applied from the Backoffice is visually distinguished from a block applied by the user themselves in the app is currently under review. When in doubt, confirm the block status with the user.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Remove a product">
    You can remove (unlink) a product from a user's account. The typical use case is when a user has created a new MyEdenred account and needs the product removed from their old account before they can add it to the new one.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the product">
        Navigate to the product in the **Cards** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Remove">
        Click the **Remove** action.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Confirm the removal. The product is immediately unlinked from the account. The physical card or wallet itself is not affected — it can be added to another account.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Removing a product from an account is immediate and cannot be undone from the Backoffice. The user will need to go through the product-addition flow again on their current account. Confirm with the user before proceeding.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Associated users

Some Edenred cards support an **associated user** (second account holder) — a person who has been granted read-only access to the primary user's card, typically a family member who wants to monitor the card balance. Wallets do not support associated users.

### Viewing associated users

Within a card's detail view, open the **Associated Users** tab. The list shows every person who currently has access to that card, including:

- Their **name** and **email**
- Their **access level** (always read-only for associated users)
- The **date** they were added

### Removing an associated user

If a user requests that an associated user be removed — for example, after a family situation changes — you can do this directly from the Backoffice:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card">
    Navigate to the relevant card in the user's **Cards** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Associated Users">
    Open the **Associated Users** tab within the card detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the user">
    Click **Remove** next to the associated user you want to unlink, then confirm the action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Backoffice allows you to view existing associated users and remove them. You cannot add new associated users on behalf of a cardholder — the primary account holder must do this themselves through the MyEdenred app.
</Note>

## What support staff cannot do

There are deliberate boundaries on what the Backoffice can do with products. The following operations are **not available** to Backoffice operators:

- **Initiate payments** on behalf of a user — you cannot make purchases or transfers using a user's card or wallet
- **Change a PIN** — PIN management is performed exclusively by the card holder within the MyEdenred app; the Backoffice has no access to PIN data
- **Process financial operations** — loading benefits, transferring balances, or reversing transactions are handled by Edenred's backend systems and must be escalated through the appropriate internal process

<Warning>
  Never attempt to work around these boundaries. If a user requests a financial operation that requires backend access, open a ticket with Edenred's operations team and inform the user of the expected resolution timeline.
</Warning>

## Financial platform limitations

The Backoffice shows product status and balance as reported by the underlying financial platforms — it does not process any financial operations itself.

| Product category | What the Backoffice can do |
|---|---|
| **Cards** (Cartão Refeição, Cartão Flexível) | View balance, view transactions, block/unblock, remove from account |
| **Wallets** (Wallet Creche, Wallet Educação) | View balance, view transactions, block/unblock, remove from account |

<Info>
  If a user reports an issue with benefit loading (credits not arriving on a card or wallet), this is managed by the employer's client portal and Edenred's operations team — not the MyEdenred Backoffice. Direct the user to contact Edenred Customer Support through the official support channel, or escalate internally if you are already on the support team.
</Info>
