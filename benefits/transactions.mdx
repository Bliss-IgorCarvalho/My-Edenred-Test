Every product in MyEdenred keeps a complete, chronological record of your activity. Whether you want to check where you spent your meal allowance last week, confirm a school payment went through, or export a receipt for an expense claim, the Transactions tab puts the full picture at your fingertips. You can narrow down a long list in seconds using filters, and download a PDF proof of payment for any individual transaction directly from the web portal.

## Accessing Your Transactions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product">
    Tap a card or wallet in the product carousel on the Benefits screen. The **Transactions** tab opens by default when you enter a product's detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the list">
    Scroll through the list to review your activity. Transactions are ordered from most recent to oldest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Each Transaction Shows

Each entry in your transaction list displays the following information:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Date** | The calendar date the transaction took place |
| **Time** | The exact time of the transaction |
| **Description** | The merchant or establishment name where the transaction occurred |
| **Amount** | The value debited from your balance (or credited, in the case of a reversal) |
| **Resulting balance** | Your remaining balance immediately after this transaction |

## Filtering Transactions

If you have a long transaction history, filters help you zero in on exactly what you're looking for without scrolling through everything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter options">
    On the Transactions tab, tap the **Filter** icon or button. A panel appears with the available filter criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your filters">
    Apply one or more of the available filters:

    - **Date range** — set a start date and end date to see only transactions within a specific period
    - **Amount** — filter by a minimum amount, a maximum amount, or a specific range
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filters">
    Tap **Apply** (or equivalent) to refresh the list. Only the transactions matching your criteria are shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear filters">
    To return to the full list, tap **Clear filters** or remove each filter individually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the date range filter alongside the amount filter to pinpoint a specific transaction — for example, a payment you know you made on a particular day for a particular amount.
</Tip>

## Downloading a Payment Receipt

For any card transaction, you can download a PDF proof of payment to keep for your records or attach to an expense report.

<Note>
  Receipt download is available on the **web portal**. If you need a receipt, open MyEdenred in your browser and navigate to the transaction in question.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Transactions tab on the web portal">
    Log in to MyEdenred via your browser, open the relevant card's detail view, and select the **Transactions** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the transaction">
    Scroll or use filters to locate the specific transaction for which you need a receipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the receipt">
    Click the **Download** icon or link next to the transaction. A PDF proof of payment is generated and saved to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Wallet Transactions vs. Top-ups

For digital wallets, your activity is split across two separate tabs to make it easier to distinguish between money going out and money coming in.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Transactions">
    The **Transactions** tab shows every **outgoing payment** you have made from the wallet — each payment to a childcare centre or school. Each entry shows the date, time, establishment name, amount paid, and resulting balance, identical to card transactions.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Top-ups">
    The **Top-ups** tab shows every **incoming load** deposited by your employer. Each entry shows:

    - **Date** the load was applied
    - **Amount** loaded
    - **Available balance** after that load
    - **Expiry date** — when those specific funds will expire if unspent

    <Warning>
      Top-up funds expire **24 months** after the load date. Keep an eye on expiry dates in this tab to make sure you use your balance before it lapses.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Permissions for Associated Users

<Note>
  If you are an **associated user** on someone else's card, you can view the full transaction history for that card in read-only mode. You cannot download receipts, apply account-level actions, or interact with any transaction — your access is limited to viewing.
</Note>

Associated users are not supported on wallets; only the wallet owner can view wallet transactions and top-ups.
