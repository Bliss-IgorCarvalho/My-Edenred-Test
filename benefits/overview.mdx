The Benefits dashboard is the heart of MyEdenred. Every product your employer has assigned to you — whether a physical card or a digital wallet — appears here in a single, unified view. From this screen you can check your current balance, dive into your transaction history, manage your card settings, and perform product-specific actions, all without leaving the app.

## The Product Carousel

When you open the Benefits section, your products are displayed in a scrollable **carousel** — one card per product. Swipe left or right to move between products. Each card in the carousel shows you, at a glance:

- **Benefit name** — for example, *Cartão Refeição* or *Wallet Creche*
- **Product type** — Card or Wallet
- **Card or Wallet number** — on mobile, the middle digits are masked for security; only the first and last digits are visible. The full number is available inside the product detail screen. On the web portal, the number is displayed in full.
- **Status** — whether the product is active, blocked, or inactive
- **Current balance** — the amount available to spend right now

Tap any product in the carousel to open its full detail view.

## Product Tabs

Once you select a product, the detail view organises all available information and actions into tabs. The tabs you see depend on your product type and your role (owner or associated user).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Card — Owner">
    If you are the primary holder of a card, you have access to the following tabs:

    | Tab | What you'll find |
    |---|---|
    | **Details** | Card number, expiry date, current balance, and status |
    | **Transactions** | Full chronological history of card movements |
    | **Associated Users** | Manage the people who have read access to your card |
    | **FAQs** | Frequently asked questions about your card benefit |
    | **Conditions** | Terms and conditions for this product |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Card — Associated User">
    If you have been granted access to someone else's card as an associated user, you can see:

    | Tab | What you'll find |
    |---|---|
    | **Details** | Card number, balance, and status (read-only) |
    | **Transactions** | Transaction history for the shared card (read-only) |
    | **FAQs** | Frequently asked questions about the card benefit |
    | **Conditions** | Terms and conditions for this product |

    <Note>
      As an associated user, you cannot block or unblock the card, change the PIN, or manage other associated users. Those actions are reserved for the card owner.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Wallet">
    For digital wallets (Wallet Creche and Wallet Educação), the owner has access to:

    | Tab | What you'll find |
    |---|---|
    | **Details** | Wallet ID, current balance, and status |
    | **Transactions** | Full chronological history of wallet payments |
    | **Top-ups** | Incoming loads from your employer, with amounts and expiry dates |
    | **Payment Profiles** | Saved establishment and student combinations for faster payments |
    | **Pay** | Make a payment directly to a childcare centre or school |
    | **FAQs** | Frequently asked questions about your wallet benefit |
    | **Conditions** | Terms and conditions for this product |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  When you first open a product detail, the **Transactions** tab is selected by default so you can immediately see your latest activity.
</Info>

## Favouriting a Product

If you use one product more than others, you can pin it to the front of the carousel so it always appears first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product detail">
    Tap the product you want to favourite in the carousel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as favourite">
    Look for the **"Set as favourite"** option in the product detail screen and tap it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the new position">
    The product now appears as the first item in your carousel whenever you open the Benefits section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can only have one favourite product at a time. Setting a new favourite automatically removes the previous one from the top position.
</Note>

## Manually Refreshing Your Products

MyEdenred keeps your data up to date automatically, but if you want to force an immediate sync — for example, right after your employer loads a new top-up — you can trigger a manual refresh.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Benefits section">
    Make sure you are on the main Benefits screen showing the product carousel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger the refresh">
    Pull down on the screen or tap the **Refresh** button. MyEdenred fetches the latest data from Edenred and reloads your current balances and product details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing a Product

If you no longer want a product to appear in your app — for example, an old card you no longer use — you can remove it from your account view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product detail">
    Tap the product you want to remove in the carousel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Remove product">
    Find the **"Remove product"** option within the product detail screen and confirm your decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Product is removed from view">
    The product disappears from your carousel immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a product from MyEdenred **does not cancel your benefit**. Your card or wallet remains active with your employer; it simply no longer appears in the app. If you removed it by mistake, you can add it again from the product association flow. If you remove your only product, your account will be in a holding state until you add a new product.
</Warning>
