Edenred wallets are fully digital benefit products — there is no physical card to carry. Instead, you make payments directly inside the MyEdenred app to authorised childcare centres and schools. Your employer loads funds into your wallet each month, and you use those funds to pay invoices in just a few taps, confirmed with a one-time SMS passcode. Wallets give you a clear, auditable record of every payment and every incoming load in one place.

## Available Wallet Products

| Wallet | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **Wallet Creche** | Childcare payments for pre-school children at enrolled nurseries and childcare centres |
| **Wallet Educação** | Education expenses for students in primary, secondary, and higher education |

## Wallet Details

Tap a wallet in the product carousel to open its detail view. The **Details** tab shows:

- **Wallet ID** — the unique identifier for your digital wallet
- **Current balance** — the total amount available to spend
- **Status** — Active or Blocked

<Note>
  Wallets have **no physical card**. You cannot use a wallet at a point-of-sale terminal or ATM. All payments happen exclusively inside the MyEdenred app. Wallets also do **not support Apple Pay or Google Pay**.
</Note>

<Note>
  Wallets do not support associated users. Only you, as the account owner, can view the balance, make payments, and manage the wallet.
</Note>

## Payment Profiles

Payment profiles save you time on repeat payments. After your first successful payment to an establishment (for example, your child's nursery), MyEdenred automatically creates a payment profile that stores the establishment name and the student or child's reference number. The next time you pay the same establishment, you simply select the profile and enter the amount — no need to search for the establishment or re-enter student details.

### Viewing your profiles

Open the **Payment Profiles** tab in your wallet detail view to see all saved profiles. Each entry shows the student or child's name and the linked establishment.

### How profiles are created

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make your first payment to an establishment">
    The first time you pay a particular childcare centre or school, you enter the student reference, search for the establishment, and complete the payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Profile is created automatically">
    After the payment succeeds, MyEdenred automatically saves a payment profile combining the establishment and student reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the profile for future payments">
    The next time you pay the same establishment, select the saved profile from the Payment Profiles tab. You only need to enter the amount — the rest is pre-filled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Removing a profile

To remove a payment profile you no longer need, open the **Payment Profiles** tab, tap the profile you want to delete, and confirm the removal. The profile is removed immediately; your transaction history is not affected.

## Making a Payment

Wallet payments are made entirely within MyEdenred. Every payment requires an SMS OTP (one-time passcode) for confirmation — this step cannot be skipped and is requested every time you pay.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pay tab">
    Tap your wallet in the carousel and select the **Pay** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter student details or select a profile">
    If you have a saved payment profile for this establishment, select it. Otherwise, enter the student or child reference number and search for the establishment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the payment amount">
    Type the amount you want to pay. The minimum payment is €0.01 and the maximum is €25,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the payment summary — establishment, student reference, and amount — then tap **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the SMS OTP">
    MyEdenred sends a one-time passcode to the mobile number registered on your account. Enter the code to authorise the payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment complete">
    Your wallet balance updates immediately and the payment appears as a new entry in your Transactions tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The SMS OTP is requested on **every single payment** — there is no session cache or "remember me" option for this step. Make sure your registered mobile number is up to date so you always receive the code.
</Warning>

## Viewing Top-ups

Your employer loads funds into your wallet periodically. You can see every incoming load in the **Top-ups** tab of your wallet detail view. Each top-up entry shows:

- **Date** the load was applied
- **Amount** added to the wallet
- **Available balance** after that load
- **Expiry date** — the date on which that particular load expires

<Warning>
  Wallet top-up funds have a **validity of 24 months (2 years)** from the date they are loaded. Any balance that has not been spent by the expiry date is removed from your wallet automatically. Check the Top-ups tab regularly to make sure you are using your funds before they expire.
</Warning>

## Blocking and Unblocking Your Wallet

Just like a card, you can block your wallet if you need to pause all outgoing payments. The block is applied to the wallet's backend immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wallet detail">
    Tap the wallet in your product carousel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Block the wallet">
    On the Details tab, select **"Block wallet"** and confirm. The wallet status changes to **Blocked** and no payments can be made until you unblock it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unblock when ready">
    Follow the same steps and select **"Unblock wallet"** to restore the wallet to **Active** status.
  </Step>
</Steps>
