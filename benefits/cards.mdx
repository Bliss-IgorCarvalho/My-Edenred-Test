Edenred cards are physical plastic cards that your employer loads with benefit funds each month. You use them directly at point-of-sale terminals — in restaurants, supermarkets, pharmacies, schools, and other participating establishments. MyEdenred gives you a digital companion for those cards: check your balance before you pay, review your spending history, protect a lost or stolen card with an instant block, and update your PIN, all from your phone or browser.

## Available Card Products

MyEdenred currently supports two card types. Both work the same way in the app; they differ in where you can spend.

| Product | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **Cartão Refeição** | Daily meal subsidies at restaurants, canteens, and supermarkets |
| **Cartão Flexível** | Healthcare, education, and social support expenses at authorised establishments |

## Card Details

When you tap a card in the product carousel, the **Details** tab shows you everything you need to know about that card:

- **Card number** — partially masked on mobile (first and last digits visible); shown in full on the web portal
- **Expiry date** — the date printed on the physical card
- **Current balance** — funds available to spend right now
- **Card status** — Active, Blocked, or Inactive

<Note>
  Your card arrives from Edenred **already activated**. You do not need to take any step in the app to activate it — just start spending at participating establishments. Adding the card to MyEdenred is optional and is only needed if you want to manage it digitally.
</Note>

## Blocking and Unblocking Your Card

If your card is lost, stolen, or you simply want to pause spending for any reason, you can block it instantly from the app. Blocking prevents any new transactions from going through at the point of sale. When you find the card or resolve the situation, you can unblock it just as quickly.

<Warning>
  Only the **card owner** can block or unblock a card. Associated users do not have this permission.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card detail">
    Tap your card in the product carousel to open its detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the block action">
    On the Details tab, tap **"Block card"**. MyEdenred sends the block instruction to the card processing backend immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the block">
    Confirm your decision when prompted. The card status updates to **Blocked** and the card cannot be used at any point of sale until you unblock it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To unblock, follow the same steps and tap **"Unblock card"** instead. The card returns to **Active** status straight away.

<Tip>
  If you suspect your card has been lost or stolen, block it immediately — it only takes a few seconds and you can always unblock it later if it turns up. There is no penalty for blocking and unblocking your card.
</Tip>

## Changing Your PIN

You can update your card PIN directly inside MyEdenred without visiting a branch or calling support.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card detail">
    Tap your card in the product carousel to open its detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access PIN settings">
    On the Details tab, select the **"Change PIN"** option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new PIN">
    Follow the on-screen prompts to enter and confirm your new PIN. Your new PIN is applied immediately and works the next time you use the card at a terminal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Associated Users

As a card owner, you can grant another person — such as a partner or family member — read access to your card. Associated users can view your balance and transaction history but cannot block the card, change the PIN, or take any other management action.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card detail">
    Tap your card in the carousel and select the **Associated Users** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an associated user">
    Tap **"Add associated user"** and enter the details of the person you want to invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage existing users">
    Existing associated users appear in a list. Tap any entry to remove their access at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Associated users are supported on **Cards only**. Digital wallets (Wallet Creche and Wallet Educação) do not support associated users — only the account owner can access a wallet.
</Note>

## Payment Method Compatibility

<Warning>
  Edenred cards are **not compatible with Apple Pay or Google Pay**. Because they operate on a proprietary payment scheme rather than an open Visa or Mastercard network, they cannot be added to a device wallet. You must always use the physical card at participating point-of-sale terminals.
</Warning>
