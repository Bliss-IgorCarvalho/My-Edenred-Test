Edenred's digital wallets — **Wallet Creche** and **Wallet Educação** — let you pay childcare centres and schools directly from the MyEdenred app or web portal, without needing a physical card. Every payment is secured with a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS to your registered mobile number, so you stay in control at all times.

## Making a Payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pay tab">
    Navigate to your wallet and tap the **Pay** tab. This tab is only available to the account owner — wallet products do not support associated users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the student number or choose a saved profile">
    Type in the student number for whom you are making the payment. If you have previously paid this establishment, you can select a saved **Payment Profile** instead and skip straight to entering the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for and select the establishment">
    Search for the school or childcare centre by name or NIF (tax identification number), then select it from the results list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the payment amount">
    Type the amount you want to pay. Payments must be between **€0.01** and **€25,000**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm the details">
    Check the student name, establishment, and amount on the confirmation screen. If everything looks correct, tap **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the SMS OTP">
    MyEdenred sends a one-time password to your registered mobile number. Enter the code when prompted to authorise the payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment confirmed">
    Once the OTP is accepted, the amount is deducted from your wallet balance and the transaction appears immediately in your payment history. If this was your first payment to this establishment, a Payment Profile is automatically saved for future use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only the account owner can make wallet payments. Wallet products do not have an associated-user role, so no one else can initiate a payment on your behalf.
</Warning>

## Payment Limits

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Minimum payment** | €0.01 |
| **Maximum payment** | €25,000 |

<Note>
  An OTP is required for every individual payment. Codes are not cached between sessions — even if you make two payments in a row, you will receive and need to enter a fresh SMS code each time.
</Note>

## Payment Profiles

A Payment Profile is a saved combination of a **student number** and an **establishment**. Once a profile exists, future payments to that establishment only require you to enter the amount — the student and destination are already filled in for you.

### How profiles are created automatically

After your first successful payment to any establishment, MyEdenred automatically creates a Payment Profile for that student-and-establishment pair. You do not need to do anything — the profile will appear in your list the next time you open the Pay tab. You can hold multiple profiles per wallet, for example if you have two children at different schools.

### Adding a profile manually

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment Profiles">
    From the wallet's Pay tab, select **Payment Profiles** to view your saved profiles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add Profile">
    Tap **Add** (or the **+** button) to open a new profile form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter the student number and search for the establishment you want to associate with this profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Confirm the details and save the profile. It will appear in your profile list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Editing an existing profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment Profiles">
    From the wallet's Pay tab, go to **Payment Profiles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Edit">
    Tap the **Edit** action next to the profile you want to update. The edit form opens in a browser view within the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the details">
    Make the necessary changes to the student number or establishment, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Deleting a profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment Profiles">
    From the wallet's Pay tab, go to **Payment Profiles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Remove">
    Tap the **Remove** action next to the profile you no longer need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Confirm the removal when prompted. The profile is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered, though you can always create a new one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set up Payment Profiles for every school or childcare centre you pay regularly. Once saved, repeat payments take only seconds — just pick the profile and enter the amount.
</Tip>
