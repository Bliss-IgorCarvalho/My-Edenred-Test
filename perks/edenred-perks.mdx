Edenred Perks are exclusive discounts and promotions from a curated selection of partner brands, available to every MyEdenred user. It doesn't matter whether you hold a Meal Card, a Flexible Card, a Childcare Wallet, or an Education Wallet — if you have an account on MyEdenred, you have full access to the entire perks catalogue. Edenred's Marketing team manages and refreshes these offers regularly, so there's always something new to discover.

## Accessing Edenred Perks

You can find the Perks section directly from the main navigation in the MyEdenred app or web portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open MyEdenred">
    Launch the MyEdenred app on your phone or sign in via the web portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Perks">
    Tap **Perks** (or **Vantagens**) in the bottom navigation bar or main menu. The full list of available perks loads immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or filter">
    Scroll through the list or select a category to narrow down offers that interest you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a perk to view details">
    Select any perk to open its full detail page and find out how to redeem it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Edenred Perks are available to **all** MyEdenred users, regardless of which product or products you hold. You do not need a specific card type to access them.
</Note>

## How Perks Are Displayed

The perks list loads as a continuous scroll — simply swipe down to browse all available offers. Each perk card in the list shows you the key information at a glance:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Partner image" icon="image">
    A featured image representing the brand or establishment offering the discount.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Promotion highlight" icon="tag">
    A short, prominent callout showing the headline offer (e.g. "20% off your next purchase").
  </Card>
  <Card title="Validity period" icon="calendar">
    The date range during which the perk is active and can be redeemed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Establishment name" icon="store">
    The name of the partner brand or establishment associated with the perk.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Brief description" icon="align-left">
    A short summary of what the perk offers, so you can decide whether to open it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Background image" icon="photo-film">
    A visual background image that gives each perk its own distinctive look.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Filtering by Category

If you're looking for offers in a specific area — such as dining, health, leisure, or shopping — you can filter the list by category.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Perks section">
    Navigate to the Perks screen as described above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a category filter">
    At the top of the list, you'll see the available categories. Tap any category to filter the perks list and show only offers in that group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the filter">
    To see all perks again, tap the active category or select **All** to remove the filter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Categories are managed by Edenred's team. If you don't see a category you expected, it may not have active offers at this time — check back later or browse the full list.
</Tip>

## Viewing Perk Details

Tapping on a perk opens its full detail page, where you'll find everything you need to take advantage of the offer.

The detail view includes:

- **Full description** — a comprehensive explanation of the promotion, including any relevant context about the partner brand.
- **Terms and conditions** — the specific conditions that apply to the offer, such as minimum spend requirements, excluded items, or geographic restrictions.
- **How to redeem** — clear instructions on what you need to do to claim the discount, whether in-store, online, or via a promotional code.
- **Images and links** — additional visuals and, where applicable, direct links to the partner's website or online store. Tapping an online store link opens it in a new browser tab.
- **Action buttons** — some perks include up to two optional buttons at the bottom of the page, such as a **Learn More** button that takes you to further information on the partner's own website or to a relevant section within the app.

<Info>
  If a perk has an associated online store, tapping the link opens the partner's website in your browser so you can shop or claim the offer directly.
</Info>

## Keeping Up With New Offers

Edenred's Marketing team updates the perks catalogue regularly — new partners are added, seasonal promotions come and go, and existing offers are refreshed. Check the Perks section frequently to make sure you don't miss out on the latest deals.

<Tip>
  **Perks are not the same as the merchant network.** The merchant network (the "Where to Use" section) shows you physical and online establishments where your Edenred card or wallet is accepted as a payment method. Perks, on the other hand, are special promotional discounts from partner brands — they represent added value on top of your everyday benefit spending.
</Tip>
